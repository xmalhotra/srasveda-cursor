# Credibility Evidence

The evidence register behind a public review, customer story, professional perspective, heritage fact or operational proof point.

## Types

- `verified-purchase-review`
- `customer-story`
- `professional-perspective`
- `heritage-record`
- `operational-proof`

## Required fields

- Source record/interview/submission, source date and accountable owner.
- Relationship verification method where relevant: completed order, signed permission, dated record or other approved evidence.
- Exact public wording/media, allowed audience/channels and publication start/end/review date.
- Consent/permission version, moderation/approval decision, reviewer and audit history.

## Rules

- A public item must be traceable back to evidence but the source record is internal and access-controlled.
- Consent is specific to the displayed identity, quote, image, location and channel; it may be withdrawn according to the approved policy.
- Withdrawal/expiry unpublishes the item from website/social queues and preserves the internal compliance history.
