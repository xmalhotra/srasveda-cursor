# Product Deck

A versioned, approved set of product information rendered as a public product page, doctor resource, or MR tablet presentation.

| Section | Public | Doctor | MR internal |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Hero images and pack information | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Approved positioning and category | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Ingredients/composition | As approved | Yes | Yes |
| Directions, storage and warnings | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Product FAQ | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Professional resource/PDF | No/linked only as approved | Yes | Yes |
| Internal talking points and sampling strategy | No | No | Yes |
| Pricing | Approved customer price only | Policy pending | Internal price context by permission |

Every section has content owner, approval status, version and review date. An expired/unapproved section cannot be shown publicly or sent by WhatsApp.
