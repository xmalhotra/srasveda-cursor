# Product Feedback and Reviews

Keep two deliberately separate types:

1. **Internal doctor feedback** — structured feedback after a sample/trial; visible only to authorised internal users unless separately approved.
2. **Customer review** — potential public testimonial; requires identity/consent, moderation, approved wording and publication status.

3. **Customer story / professional perspective** — longer-form evidence, kept separate from a product review. It requires source interview/submission, explicit scope-specific publication consent, review of every quote/image/name/location and a verified link to the claimed relationship/product where applicable.

No unmoderated feedback or patient outcome claim can appear on a public product page. Review records retain source, date, consent status, moderation decision, approved display copy and related product.

## Public credibility rules

- A `verified purchase` label is available only when the review is linked to a completed/delivered customer order; it is not a quality or medical-efficacy certification.
- Negative reviews are not rejected merely because they are negative. Moderation removes/reworks only content that is irrelevant, abusive, private, unverifiable, unlawful or makes an unapproved claim; the decision and reason are retained internally.
- A customer story describes the person’s genuine product/service experience and relationship with Srasveda. It cannot claim cure, guaranteed result, diagnosis/treatment outcome or act as a substitute for professional advice.
- A doctor/clinic story is a professional perspective and may only state the specifically approved, permissioned factual relationship. It cannot imply network-wide endorsement or reveal a private partner relationship.
