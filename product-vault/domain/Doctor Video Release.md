# Doctor Video Release

The clip-by-clip consent, verification and approval record required before a doctor video can appear on the Srasveda website or social channels.

## Required evidence

- Linked verified [[domain/Doctor]] profile and date of verification.
- Original video, final edited version, full transcript and captions.
- Exact product/SKU/category and permitted factual subject matter.
- Signed consent covering name, qualifications, image/voice, clinic/location, website/social channels, edits, duration and withdrawal/contact process.
- Payment/benefit disclosure and conflict-of-interest record, where applicable.
- Content/compliance/legal reviewer, approved extracts, approval date and expiry/review date.

## Decisions

`received → rights/identity review → transcript review → approved → published → expired/withdrawn/rejected`

## Publication guardrails

- The video must be reviewed after final editing; an approved raw recording does not approve a later cut, caption or thumbnail.
- No private partner-network, patient/customer, MR/task, internal pricing or medical record content may appear.
- Consent withdrawal/expiry triggers prompt website removal and an auditable social-platform removal/escalation task.
