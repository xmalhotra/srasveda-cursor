# Product Price

Pricing is versioned and audience-specific. It must not be hard-coded into a product page.

Possible price contexts: public/customer MRP, customer selling price, doctor/clinic agreement, distributor price and temporary campaign price. Each record needs currency, effective dates, audience/eligibility, approval owner and product/variant scope.
