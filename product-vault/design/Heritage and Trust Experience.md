# Heritage and Trust Experience

## Homepage placement

```text
Hero: What Srasveda is today + Explore products

Quiet heritage line: Established in 1998 | Read our story

Products: clear images, approved information and price

Why customers choose Srasveda:
  Transparent product information | Human support | Established heritage

Support / delivery / return / privacy links
```

Heritage is intentionally calm. The hero should lead with relevance and product clarity; the 1998 story earns confidence beneath it rather than becoming an unsupported boast.

## Our-story page

```text
Established in 1998
One-paragraph approved origin story

Timeline: only verified milestones

How we work today
  Product stewardship | Professional relationships | Customer support

Need support? Official WhatsApp
```

## Product-page trust block

```text
Product image + price
What it is | Ingredients/composition | Directions | Warnings
Verified product/manufacturer details where appropriate
Customer reviews (moderated)
Support | Delivery / returns | Need a consultation?
```

## Evidence register template

For every visible trust element, record internally:

| Public element | Evidence source | Owner | Approval | Review date |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Established in 1998 | Founding/registration/archival record | Founder | Pending | Pending |
| Timeline milestone | Dated source record | Brand owner | Pending | Pending |
| Doctor quote/logo | Written permission + approved copy | Compliance | Pending | Pending |
| Customer review | Consent + moderation record | Support/brand | Pending | Pending |
| Manufacturing/licence detail | SKU-specific verified record | Regulatory owner | Pending | Pending |
```

## Tone

- Warm, precise, established, quietly confident.
- Use tangible dates, places, people and product facts once verified.
- Avoid over-designed seals, stock “doctor” imagery, unsupported counters and dramatic health promises.
