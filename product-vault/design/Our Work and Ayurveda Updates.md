# Our Work and Ayurveda Updates

## Purpose

Give customers a calm, credible view of what Srasveda is doing and the official Ayurveda ecosystem it follows—without turning the page into a news feed, political commentary or a claim page.

## Page structure

```text
Our Work & Ayurveda Updates

Srasveda in practice
  Curated cards: dated activity, short factual summary, approved image
  Read more / relevant official Srasveda page

Ayurveda and official initiatives
  Curated cards: initiative title, neutral summary, source label/date
  Read the original source →

Follow Srasveda
  Official Instagram | Official X
```

## Design/content rules

- Show a finite, selected set of cards—not an automatic, infinite social/news feed.
- Visibly separate `Srasveda in practice` from `Official initiative` content.
- Every official card names and links to its primary source.
- Do not use government logos/emblems, call a government initiative “our initiative,” or place a product CTA beside a source item in a way that suggests endorsement.
- The page is an informational/brand-trust surface; it carries no diagnosis, treatment promise or customer/partner data.
