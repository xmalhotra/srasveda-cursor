# Reviews and Customer Stories

## Product page

```text
Customer reviews
  [Verified purchase] First name, city (only if consented) | Month/year
  Rating + approved factual review copy
  Read moderation policy | Need support?
```

## Brand trust page

```text
Customer stories
  First name/identity as consented | Dated, factual experience
  Link to relevant product/support context where appropriate

Professional perspectives
  Only record-specific, permissioned statements and approved doctor videos

How we collect and moderate reviews
```

## Design rules

- Never lead with a dramatic claim or before/after image.
- `Verified purchase` explains the purchase connection; it is not a seal of efficacy.
- Keep the person’s words specific, short and attributed only to the consented extent.
- Link to full product information, directions/warnings and support—not an aggressive buy-now CTA beside a personal story.
- Doctor videos show captions/transcript and a neutral individual-perspective label; they do not link to a doctor/clinic directory.
