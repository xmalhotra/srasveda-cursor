# REV-001 — Reviews and Credibility Evidence

## User outcome

Visitors see credible, relevant human evidence behind Srasveda: real customer experiences, carefully permissioned professional perspectives and verifiable operational facts. Nothing is anonymous hype, invented success or a disguised medical claim.

## Evidence ladder

| Level | Public form | Required proof |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Verified-purchase review | Delivered order linked to the reviewer, consent and moderation |
| 2 | Customer story | Original interview/submission, specific publication consent, approved copy/media and relevant relationship/order verification |
| 3 | Professional perspective | Written permission, verified identity/relationship, approved factual wording and no implied network-wide endorsement |
| 4 | Operational proof | Source document for heritage, product/process/support fact and named approval owner |

## Customer review flow

1. After a delivered order and an approved delay, invite the customer to rate product/service and optionally add a review.
2. The customer sees a clear consent choice for publication and display identity: first name only, first name/city, or anonymous where policy allows.
3. The review enters moderation. It is linked to the delivered order, but staff do not publish order details.
4. Approved reviews may display on the relevant product page with date and `Verified purchase` label where applicable.
5. Rejected/edited/withdrawn reviews retain a reason and audit history; a negative review is not rejected merely because it is negative.

## Customer stories and professional perspectives

- Use the public label **Customer story** or **Professional perspective**, not “success story,” unless legal/compliance approves the exact content/category.
- Stories must say what the person genuinely experienced about product/service/support in approved non-medical terms.
- Collect written permission separately for quote, full/partial name, city, photo/video and website/social channels.
- Doctor/clinic participation is opt-in, record-specific and never becomes a searchable network listing or blanket “recommended by doctors” claim.

## Doctor video perspectives

- A doctor video is treated as a high-risk `professional perspective`, not a general product asset or automatically valid endorsement.
- Each individual clip requires: verified doctor identity/professional details, signed release, final transcript/captions, exact product/category scope, usage channels (website/social), edit permission, expiry/withdrawal terms and content/compliance approval.
- Approved public wording must remain factual and non-medical. Claims of cure, treatment, prevention, guaranteed result, superiority, disease/condition outcome, or “every doctor recommends” are blocked unless the applicable legal/regulatory review approves the exact wording.
- Start with a small curated set after approval rather than a wall of 15 videos. Place them on a clearly labelled `Professional perspectives` page, not beside a purchase button as if each were a blanket clinical endorsement.
- Every published video displays captions and an accurate transcript; the name, qualifications, clinic/location and image are shown only to the consented extent.

## Moderation rules

- Remove/rewrite private data, abusive content, false identity, unsupported treatment/cure/guarantee claims, before/after medical promises and content outside the approved product category.
- Do not selectively publish only positive stories while representing a numerical rating as complete. If an aggregate rating is displayed, define its population, period and inclusion rules transparently.
- Do not pay for or incentivise a positive rating. Any incentive to submit feedback must be neutral and disclosed according to the approved policy.
- The review/stories page links to a short moderation policy and official Support route.

## Publication and withdrawal

- Each item needs an approved [[domain/Credibility Evidence]] record and follows [[domain/Content Publication]].
- Consent expiry/withdrawal triggers a review/unpublish task; social posts cannot be silently altered after a platform publish, so the removal/escalation policy must be followed.
- Website cards can be removed promptly by authorised publisher; all actions are audited.

## Acceptance tests

- A `verified purchase` label cannot appear without a linked delivered order.
- A public review cannot reveal order details, phone number, health data or unapproved medical claim.
- A moderator cannot reject a review solely because its rating is negative; a reason category is required.
- A customer story cannot publish without channel-specific consent for every displayed identifier/media item.
- A doctor/clinic logo/quote cannot publish without written permission and approved scope.
- A doctor video cannot publish without a completed [[domain/Doctor Video Release]] and transcript-level approval.
- Withdrawing consent removes eligible website content and creates an auditable social-removal task.
