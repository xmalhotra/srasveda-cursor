# PUB-001 — Heritage and Trust Experience

## User outcome

A visitor understands that Srasveda has been operating since 1998, sees credible reasons to trust the brand, and can verify the product/support information needed to make a purchase—without being pushed by exaggerated medical claims.

## Core public message

**“Srasveda — established in 1998.”**

This is a heritage statement, not evidence that every product is clinically proven or suitable for every person. Each supporting statement needs a named source and approval before publication.

## Trust surfaces

### 1. Heritage strip on public home and product pages

- A restrained `Established 1998` mark, linked to the full story—not a decorative badge with no evidence.
- One short approved statement about the company’s origin and continuing focus.
- Never use a heritage date in a way that implies government certification, clinical endorsement or product efficacy.

### 2. “Our story” evidence page

- A factual timeline: founding, major approved milestones, and today’s operating model.
- Founder/organisation photographs only where rights and captions are approved.
- A small “what we believe / how we work” section: practitioner relationships, product stewardship and customer support, stated factually.
- Every milestone is backed by an internal evidence record; public copies need not disclose sensitive commercial details.

### Confirmed evidence inventory to verify/upload

- Company-establishment document and GST document.
- Evidence of Srasveda's relationship/history with established hospitals or clinics, only for the factual scope and public permissions available.
- Founder record for Dr Neeru Malhotra: founding role and Ayurveda qualification/expertise evidence, plus permission/approved wording for public presentation.
- Internal sales evidence of approximately ₹50 lakh worth of products sold. This is useful as internal business proof; it must not become a public sales/revenue claim unless finance/brand approves the exact period, basis and wording.

### 3. Product-page proof block

- Product/SKU name, pack images, category, ingredient/composition information, directions, warnings and approved price.
- Manufacturer/licence or other applicable regulatory details only when verified for that SKU and appropriate to display.
- Link to approved FAQs, delivery/returns, support and consultation-request path.
- The source of each image, review, claim and document is traceable in the [[domain/Product]] approval workflow.

### 4. Real human proof, with controls

- Moderated customer reviews labelled as customer experiences; consent, date and publication approval required.
- Doctor/clinic quote, photograph, name or logo only with explicit written permission and approved wording.
- Do not inflate “network” counts, imply endorsement from every connected doctor, or reveal partner clinics as proof.

### 5. Operational trust

- Clear official WhatsApp support route, contact identity, order/payment/delivery information and returns/cancellation policy.
- Secure OTP account access and visible privacy/consent controls.
- If consultation is offered, describe it as a request to connect with an available partner—not as a diagnostic or “best doctor” service.

## Content guardrails

- No cure, treatment, guaranteed-result, miracle or self-diagnosis language unless the applicable category/regulatory review explicitly permits it.
- Never use “doctor recommended”, “clinically proven”, “trusted by X doctors”, “government approved” or a clinic logo without record-specific substantiation and approval.
- A review/testimonial cannot make a prohibited claim indirectly.
- Every public fact has: source/evidence reference, owner, approval state, approved wording, category/SKU scope and review date.

## Acceptance tests

- `Established 1998` links to a factual history page with approved evidence-backed milestones.
- A product page cannot publish an unapproved claim, asset, review, quote or licence/manufacturer detail.
- A doctor/clinic name or logo cannot be publicly displayed without its stored permission and approved wording.
- A visitor can find official support, price, delivery/return and privacy information without login.
- Partner routing never becomes a public network list through a heritage/trust page.
