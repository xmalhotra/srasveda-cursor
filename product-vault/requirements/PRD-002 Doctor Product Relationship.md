# PRD-002 Doctor–Product Relationship

## User outcome

An MR sees, on the doctor profile, which products have been introduced, sampled, received feedback, ordered regularly, paused or never presented—and what the next product action is.

## Guardrails

- The relationship is doctor/product-specific and history is retained.
- It does not imply a medical prescription, clinical endorsement or patient result.
- An MR sees relationships only for assigned doctors.
- State changes link to their source visit, sample, feedback or order where available.
