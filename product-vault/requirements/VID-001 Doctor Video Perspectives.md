# VID-001 — Doctor Video Perspectives

## User outcome

Visitors may view a small curated set of genuine, permissioned professional perspectives that add confidence in Srasveda’s responsible approach, without mistaking the videos for medical advice, a product guarantee or universal doctor endorsement.

## Intake and approval checklist per video

- [ ] Identify the doctor and verify the linked professional profile.
- [ ] Obtain written, channel-specific release for website and any intended social channels.
- [ ] Record whether any payment, gift, sample or other benefit exists and complete required disclosure review.
- [ ] Produce the final edit, thumbnail, full transcript and captions.
- [ ] Map each statement to permitted product/category/public wording.
- [ ] Remove/decline disease-treatment, cure, guaranteed-result, superiority, patient-identifying, private-network or unsupported claims.
- [ ] Obtain brand/content/compliance approval and legal/regulatory approval where required.
- [ ] Set publication/review/expiry date and withdrawal contact.

## Presentation

- Page title: `Professional perspectives` or other compliance-approved label.
- Each card shows only consented identity/qualification/location, short approved context, captions and transcript.
- Use a neutral context line such as “Individual professional perspective; not medical advice.” Final wording is subject to review.
- Do not use video counts or a doctor montage to imply that the whole network endorses Srasveda.
- Do not position a video as a substitute for product directions/warnings or a consultation.

## Acceptance tests

- No video can be selected in the CMS unless its [[domain/Doctor Video Release]] is approved and current.
- The public video uses the approved final file, thumbnail, captions and transcript version.
- Withdrawing consent removes the website video and creates a social-removal task.
- A product claim in transcript review blocks publication until resolved/approved.
- A user cannot browse from video cards to a directory of doctors/clinics.
